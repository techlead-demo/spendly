Wireframe (SPD-3). Shown here: the modal dialog after "Delete" was clicked on project-context/design/screens/expense-form-edit.html. The form behind is dimmed and cannot be used; focus starts on "Keep" and Tab cycles between the two dialog buttons. "Keep", Escape or a click on the dimmed area closes the dialog, keeps the field values and returns focus to "Delete". "Delete expense" deletes the expense and returns to the expense list with the undo toast (expense-list-undo-toast.html). While the delete request runs, both buttons are disabled and Escape and outside clicks are ignored. If it fails, the dialog closes and the form shows the error banner of expense-form-errors.html at the top of the card with "Could not delete the expense. Try again."
